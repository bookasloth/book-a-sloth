# Your profile and email preferences

## Updating your details

Your name, email, and phone number live on your [Profile](/profile) page. Sign in with your phone number, open Profile, and edit them there.

Keeping your email and phone current matters — that's where your booking confirmations and reminders go.

## Stopping marketing emails

Every marketing email has an **unsubscribe** link at the bottom. Click it, or visit the [unsubscribe](/unsubscribe) page, to opt out. You can resubscribe any time.

Unsubscribing stops marketing and newsletter emails. You'll still get essential messages about your actual bookings — confirmations, reminders, and reschedule or cancellation notices — because those are about services you booked, not promotions.

## Deleting your account

If you want your account removed, contact us from the [Help](/help) page and we'll handle it — this isn't something we do automatically, so a real person will confirm it with you first.
