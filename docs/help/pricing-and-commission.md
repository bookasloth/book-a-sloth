# Pricing and commission

Full, current prices and limits are always on the [pricing](/pricing) page — this is an overview of how it works.

## The plans

- **Starter** — free forever, no credit card, with real online payment collection included.
- **Host+** — a flat annual price, higher limits, and extra features.
- **Studio** — the top tier, with unlimited services and bookings plus advanced tools.

## Commission

- On **paid plans (Host+ and Studio), commission is 0%** — you keep the full service price your client pays.
- On the **free Starter plan**, a small commission applies per booking. That's what funds a genuinely free tier instead of a limited trial.

The exact percentage and prices are on the [pricing](/pricing) page.

## Payment-gateway fee

One separate cost applies on every paid booking regardless of plan: the payment gateway's own processing fee (Razorpay's, roughly 2.4%). That's charged by the gateway, not by us, and it's disclosed upfront — there are no other hidden fees.

## Switching plans

You can upgrade or downgrade as your business changes, with no lock-in beyond the billing period you've already paid for. See [Plans and upgrading](/help/docs/plans-and-upgrading).
