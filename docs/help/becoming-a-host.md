# Becoming a host

Hosts (also called creators) are the people who publish services and take bookings on Book A Sloth. Setting up is a short guided flow.

## Sign in with Google

Hosts sign in with **Google** — there's no separate password to create or remember. Start from the [Be a Sloth](/be-a-sloth) page and continue with your Google account. If you already booked something as a guest, you can turn that into a host account the same way.

Using Google also lets you connect your Google Calendar later, so your bookings land on the calendar you already use.

## The setup steps

A guided setup walks you through everything you need to go live:

1. **Claim your username** — this becomes your public link, `bookasloth.com/your-name`.
2. **Verify your WhatsApp number** — so booking alerts and confirmations reach you.
3. **Set up your profile** — photo, bio, and what you do.
4. **Set your working hours** — when you're available to be booked.
5. **Add your first service** — what you offer, how long it takes, and the price.
6. **Choose a plan** — start free on Starter, or pick a paid plan for more.

Once that's done, your page is live and bookable.

## Finish the setup to reach your dashboard

Until setup is complete, you'll be guided back to the step you left off at — you can't reach the full dashboard until your page is ready to take bookings. Pick up where you left off any time by signing in.

Stuck on a step? Reach us from the [Help](/help) page.
