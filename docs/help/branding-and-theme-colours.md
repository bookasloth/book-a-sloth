# Branding and theme colours

Your public page can reflect your brand rather than looking like a generic tool.

## Theme colours

Set your page's theme colour from your dashboard **Profile**. The range of colours you can pick from depends on your plan — the free tier has a set of standard colours, and paid plans unlock more, up to full custom colours on the top tier.

## "This colour isn't available on your plan"

You've picked a colour beyond what your current plan includes. Either choose one from your plan's set, or upgrade to unlock more — see [Plans and upgrading](/help/docs/plans-and-upgrading).

If you enter a custom colour and it's rejected, make sure it's a valid hex value like `#FE5100`.

## Custom branding

Paid plans let your booking page carry your own branding, so it looks like *your* business — useful if you're building a personal brand and don't want an obviously third-party page. Higher tiers include more branding control. The [pricing](/pricing) page shows what each plan includes.
