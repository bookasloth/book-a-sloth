# Your username and booking link

## Your link

Your username is your public booking link: `bookasloth.com/your-name`. It's what you share with clients, and it's how guests find your page and services.

Pick something clean and recognizable — usernames are at least 3 characters, and use letters, numbers, or hyphens.

## Changing your username

You can change your username, but it's deliberately limited so your link stays stable for the people who've saved it:

- You can change it **at most twice per year.**
- Your **WhatsApp number must be verified** to make a change.
- Once you release a username, it's retired and can't be reused — by you or anyone else.

Change it from your dashboard **Profile**. If you've hit the yearly limit or changed it very recently, you'll be asked to wait before changing again.

## "Why can't I change my username?"

Usually one of the above: your WhatsApp isn't verified yet, or you've already used your changes for the year. Verify WhatsApp first, and check whether you've changed it recently.

Need a change you can't make yourself? Reach us from the [Help](/help) page.
