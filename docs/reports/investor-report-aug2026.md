## 4 · Product — 170 changes shipped in 25 days
- **Sales CRM, end-to-end** — auto-created leads, scoring + intent, Kanban pipeline, WhatsApp/email sequences. The machine to close the activation gap.
- **Payments & payout rail live** — Razorpay Route, deferred KYC, gap-free GST invoicing, ledger hardening.
- **Onboarding redesign** — 4 screens rebuilt, first service created on the "You're live" screen, host-loss bugs fixed.
- **Activation & positioning** — funnel analytics, earnings-triggered upgrade nudges, weekly host recap email; site-wide "a booking link that gets you paid."

## Transparency — what we disclose (and why)
1. **Real revenue vs test volume kept separate** — headline ₹2,039 subs; booking GMV labeled pilot. Blending overstates revenue ~20×.
2. **Activation gap shown openly** (146 leads → 20 signups → 4 paying) — a solvable funnel the CRM was built to fix beats a hidden drop-off.
3. **Stage stated plainly** — infrastructure-complete, pre-transaction-scale; this quarter's value is the rail that de-risks next quarter's revenue ramp.
